# Emaki — Account & Data Deletion Policy

**Application:** Emaki  
**Package Name:** `com.emaki.reader`  

---

## How to Request Account Deletion
You can permanently delete your account and all associated cloud data directly within the Emaki Android application at any time:
1. Open the **Emaki** app.
2. Tap the **Profile** tab in the bottom navigation bar.
3. Tap the **Settings (Gear icon)** in the top-right corner.
4. Scroll to the **Account** card.
5. Tap **Delete Account & Data**.
6. Confirm the deletion prompt.

Upon confirmation, your profile, authentication credentials, saved bookmarks, follows, and local caches are **immediately and irreversibly erased** from both our servers and your device.
